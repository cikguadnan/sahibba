# Third-party dictionary notice

The built-in Malay word list is derived from **MyEja**, a Bahasa Malaysia spell-checking dictionary maintained at https://github.com/xanda/MyEja.

MyEja credits the MIMOS Open Source Group, Profesor Abdullah Hassan (UPSI), Puan Ainon Mohd. (PTS), and project contributors. Its initial dictionary is distributed under the GNU Free Documentation License. Sahibba expands MyEja entries using its published Malay affix rules, normalises them to uppercase, removes duplicates, and retains teacher review as a correction layer.
